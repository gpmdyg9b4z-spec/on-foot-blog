About This Record

These are notes from guided walking tours, set down while the detail is still fresh. They are not academic accounts, nor official guides. They are a record of what was said, what was noticed, and what lingered.

Each post follows a walk stop by stop, draws on the guide’s knowledge and the recording of the day, and is written for the reader who was there as much as the one who was not.

How the notes are made

Most walks are recorded and transcribed, then written up with the help of Claude, an AI model, and checked against the transcript. Guides’ words are quoted as closely as the recording allows. Dates, names and figures are given as the guide gave them; where a claim is disputed or a popular story doesn’t hold up, the post says so. Errors of attribution are mine, not the guide’s.

The archive

The walks run from 2022 to the present. Most are in London — Open House weekends, heritage groups, volunteer guides and the occasional laboratory — with others from Kingston, Hastings and Mersea Island, and further afield: Algiers, Tipaza, Tunis, Bordeaux, Kraków, Stockholm, Helsinki, Chișinău, Sirmione, Kathmandu, Jakarta and Komodo. Tours are added as they are taken.

The photographs

I’m a London-based editorial photographer and a Getty Images contributor, and most of these walks are also photographed. Where a post includes pictures, each one links to its page on Getty Images, where it can be licensed.
